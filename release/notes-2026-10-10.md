Unofficial OrangeFox R12.0 for **Google Pixel 4 XL (coral)**.

This is the exact image tested on Coral with Infinity Android 17, built from commit `5e77df717d12e4527b0d412bf3dd5bd9f484f43e` in [run 38071785744](https://github.com/its-hecker/orangefox_device_google_coral/actions/runs/38071785744).

Tested: boot, touch, ADB, MTP, PIN/data decryption, automatic battery percentage, settings, brightness changes and theme reload. The supplied logs show no recovery restart during the captured session. ROM ZIP installation and longer-running stability remain untested.

Temporary boot on **coral**:
```sh
fastboot getvar product
fastboot boot OrangeFox-unofficial-coral.img
```
